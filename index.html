How the environment works

  1. The environment shows one transaction (the observation).
  2. The agent may investigate or request documents (costs a small penalty, reveals information) and then must approve or flag.
  3. The environment compares the decision with the hidden truth and returns a reward.
  4. After the last transaction the episode ends and the metrics are computed.
